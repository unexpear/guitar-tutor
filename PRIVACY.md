# StandardTune Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 21, 2026

StandardTune is a free, open source guitar tuner and learning app. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: **we collect nothing.**

## Microphone

StandardTune uses your device's microphone for exactly one purpose: detecting the pitch of your guitar while the tuner or a play-along practice drill is active.

- Audio is analyzed **on your device, in real time, in memory only** (the app's built-in pitch-detection engine).
- Audio is **never recorded, never stored, and never transmitted** anywhere.
- The microphone is only active while you are on a screen that uses it and have started listening; there is no background microphone access.
- You can revoke microphone permission at any time in Android settings; every feature except the tuner and play-along drills works without it.

## Data stored on your device

Your lesson progress, quiz scores, questionnaire answers, and app settings (volume, practice goal, and similar preferences) are stored **locally on your device only**. They are never uploaded, synced, or shared, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Data collection, sharing, and tracking

- StandardTune does **not** collect any personal data.
- It contains **no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no tracking** of any kind.
- It makes **no network requests** in normal operation and has no server.
- It does **not** collect data from children, or from anyone else.

## Third parties

No data is shared with third parties, because no data ever leaves your device.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes any of the above (for example, by adding optional crash reporting), this policy will be updated first and the change will be noted in the release notes. The current version of this policy always lives at:

https://github.com/unexpear/guitar-tutor/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
