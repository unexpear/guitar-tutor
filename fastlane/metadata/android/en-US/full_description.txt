<b>StandardTune</b> is a free and open source guitar tuner and learning companion.

Features:
- Real-time tuner with native pitch detection that guides you to each string's target
- Realistic headstock visual with per-string in-tune indicators
- 31 tuning presets for guitars, basses, ukulele and chromatic tuning, with calibrated reference notes
- Interactive chord library with finger positions, diagrams, and audio playback
- Play-along practice that listens to your guitar: pluck the shown string or strum the shown chord, with an easy any-tone mode and a strict full-chord mode
- Structured lessons from beginner to advanced, with an interactive anatomy quiz
- Song chord references with tap-to-hear shapes and original microphone-scored chord practice
- Chord Quiz and one-minute Chord Changes games, with local best scores
- Built-in metronome with tap tempo, accented downbeats, and multiple time signatures
- Dark theme optimized for low-light environments

Audio is processed entirely on your device: nothing is recorded, stored, or transmitted. Fully free and open source with no ads, no tracking, and no in-app purchases.
