<b>StandardTune</b> is a free and open source guitar tuner and learning companion.

Features:
- Real-time guitar tuner with YIN pitch detection algorithm
- GuitarTuna-style headstock visual with animated string feedback
- 16 tuning presets (Standard, Drop D, Open G, DADGAD, and more)
- Interactive chord library with finger positions and audio playback
- Structured lessons from beginner to advanced
- Fun mini-games to practice chord recognition and ear training
- Built-in metronome with customizable tempo and time signatures
- Dark theme optimized for low-light environments

Built with React Native and Expo. Fully free and open source with no ads, no tracking, and no in-app purchases.
